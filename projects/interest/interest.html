I love reading because it gives me a chance to relax, learn new things and escape into a different worlds. Whenever I have free time, i enjoy picking up a good book and getting lost in the story. Reading allows me to use my imagination and experience different characters, places and adventures without ever leaving my home.

My favorite books to read are mystery books because I love the excitement of trying to figure out what is going to happen next. I enjoy looking for clues, making guesses and trying to solve the mystery before the characters do. Sometimes I think I have the answer figured out, but then the story surprises me with something i did not expect. That is what makes mystery books so fun and keeps me wanting to read more.
