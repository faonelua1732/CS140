My Hero - Sara Blakely

Hero Interest
Sara Blakely is my hero because i loved how she never gives up but always follow her dreams. Sara Blakely is the owner and founder of Spanx, a global shapewear and apparel brand. Well, her business is not the reason why i love her but it's her determination, hardwork and choosing not to give up. She did not start with a business degree or a wealthy background but she refused to quit. Now she is one of the most richest woman in the world today.
