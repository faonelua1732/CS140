My Hero: Agatha Christie

Hero Interest

Agatha Christie is my hero because she became one of the most famous mystery writers in the world. I admire her creativity and the way she created stories taht encourage readers to look for clues and solve mysteries. Her books have interesting characters and unexpected events that keep readers interested. Her work has also influenced mystery stories and readers around the world.

Hercule Poirot
Hercule Poirot is one of Agatha Christie's most famous detective characters. He appears in many of her mysteries stories an uses careful thinking to solve difficult cases.
Miss Marple
Miss Marple is another famous detective created by Agatha. She is an observant woman who uses her knowledge of people and human behavior to solve mystery.
Murder on the Orient Express
This is one of Argatha's well known mystery novels. Hercule Poirot investigates a murder that happenes while passengers are traveling on a train.
