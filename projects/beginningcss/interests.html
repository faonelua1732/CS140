Hero Interest

One of my favorite interests is reading, especially mystery books.

I enjoy mystery stories becaue they keep me curious and make me want to continue reading to find out what happens. I like trying to figure out the mystery before the characters reveal the answer. Reading also gives me a chance to relax and enjoy a story.
