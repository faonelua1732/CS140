What is the difference between block tags and inline tags?

Block tags start on a new line and fill the full horizontal widt of their parent container. Examples includes div, p, h1-h6 tags. Whereas, Inline tags stays on the same line alongside surrounding text taking up only what their content needs. Examples include span, a, strong, img tags.
