What goes in the header?

The header contains the things like an introduction, title, logo or name of the website.

What goes in the Nav? Can the Nav go in the header? Does it have to go in the header? Does the Nav contain links to another website?

The nav are the links progammers use to help users move around the website. For instance, Nav can go inside the header and can link to other pages on the same website.

What goes in Main?

The main is where the main content of the webpage goes. It can includes text, images or information that is mainly about the page.

What goes in the Footer?

The footer is mostly at the bottom of the page which include information about copyright, contact information or additional links.
