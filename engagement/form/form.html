The form tag has an action attribute. What does it do?

The action attribute tells the browser where to send the form information after the user clicks Submit. It usually points to page or program that processes the answers.

The form tag has a method attribute. What does it do?

The method attribute tells the broswer how to send the form information. The two most common methods are GET and POST.

What is the difference between a POST and a GET?

GET puts the form answers in the web addresses. POST sends the answer in the request body so they do not appear in the address bar.

How do you fill in a form control with a pre-set value, and how is that different from a placeholder?

A preset value is already entered or selected. A placeholder is only a hint, it disappears when the user strats typing and is not submitted as the answer.
